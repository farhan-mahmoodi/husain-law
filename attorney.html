Founder & Managing Attorney

Daniyal Husain, Esq.

Daniyal Husain brings a unique perspective and rigorous analytical background to his private practice.

He began his legal career serving as a tax attorney for the Internal Revenue Service (IRS), where he spent years navigating complex regulatory structures from the inside. Today, he leverages that high-stakes federal experience to defend and advise individuals and businesses throughout Maryland.

Daniyal earned his undergraduate degree in Political Science from the University of Maryland Baltimore County (UMBC) before graduating with his Juris Doctor from the University of Baltimore (UB) School of Law. Whether managing high-stakes civil litigation, personal injury recovery, or complex corporate structuring, his practice is defined by fierce advocacy and accessible, tailored solutions.
