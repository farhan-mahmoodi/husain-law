Tax Law & Controversy

Driven by insider IRS experience to handle corporate structuring, audits, compliance, and strategic federal and state tax relief.

Daniyal Husain began his career as a tax attorney for the Internal Revenue Service. That experience shapes how the firm approaches every tax matter: how the agency evaluates a position, what it looks for in an examination, and where a dispute can be resolved.

The firm advises on structuring and compliance before problems arise, and represents clients when a controversy with the IRS or the State of Maryland is already under way.

Representative matters

  • IRS and Maryland audits and examinations
  • Federal and state tax relief
  • Corporate and entity tax structuring
  • Compliance reviews
  • Responding to notices and correspondence
  • Negotiation with taxing authorities

Personal Injury

Dedicated representation for victims of accidents, fighting insurance companies to secure the medical care and financial recovery you deserve.

After an accident, the insurance company has adjusters and counsel working from the first day. You should have an advocate on your side as well.

The firm handles communication with insurers, builds the record of your injuries and losses, and pursues the compensation needed for medical care and recovery, by negotiation or in court.

Representative matters

  • Motor vehicle accidents
  • Premises liability
  • Insurance claim disputes and denials
  • Negotiation with insurance carriers
  • Recovery of medical expenses and lost income
  • Litigation when a fair settlement is refused

Civil Litigation

Strategic courtroom advocacy for individuals and businesses dealing with breach of contract, financial disputes, and tort claims.

Litigation is won through preparation. Each dispute is evaluated candidly at the outset, a strategy is set against your goals, and it is pursued with discipline, whether that leads to an early resolution or to trial.

Clients are kept informed at every stage, with the costs and risks of each path explained plainly.

Representative matters

  • Breach of contract claims and defense
  • Financial and business disputes
  • Tort claims and defense
  • Pre-suit demands and negotiation
  • Motions practice and trial
  • Settlement and enforcement

Commercial Transactions

End-to-end legal support for Maryland businesses, including entity formation, contract drafting, asset purchases, and liability management.

From formation through growth, the firm serves as counsel to Maryland businesses that need practical, well-drafted agreements and attention to liability and tax exposure.

Because transactional work is paired with tax experience, the structure of a deal is considered alongside its terms.

Representative matters

  • Entity formation and governance documents
  • Contract drafting, review and negotiation
  • Asset purchases and sales
  • Liability management
  • Operating, shareholder and partnership agreements
  • Ongoing outside counsel
