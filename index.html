The firm

At Husain Law LLC, our goal is to provide exceptional legal representation with a focus on tailoring solutions to meet our clients' unique needs. We aim to help you navigate the complexities of the legal system and keep elite representation accessible to our community.

The firm is led by Daniyal Husain, who began his career as a tax attorney for the Internal Revenue Service and now represents individuals and businesses throughout Maryland. Clients work directly with him from the first conversation to the resolution of their matter.

Practice areas

  • Tax Law & Controversy

  • Personal Injury
